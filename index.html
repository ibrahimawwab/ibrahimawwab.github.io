01 / Introduction

Two disciplines.
One evidence-led approach.

Exploring the shared responsibility of healthcare, science and technology: accuracy, trust and public benefit.

I'm Ibrahim Awwab, a medical microbiologist and cybersecurity specialist. My interests and work span diagnostic microbiology, medical laboratory sciences, cybersecurity and digital forensics.

I hold a PhD in Microbiology and an MSc in Clinical Microbiology from Queen Mary University of London, as well as an MSc in Cyber Security from the University of London. Alongside medical science and education, I focus on responsible cybersecurity practices that support resilient healthcare systems and informed communities.

This website brings together my professional interests, educational initiatives, open-source development and public profiles in one place.

04 / Biography

A professional story across science and security.

Originally from Afghanistan and based in Greater London, I work across healthcare sciences, teaching and technology. I founded Awwab Academy to support accessible learning in medical laboratory science and cybersecurity.

My interests include improving how scientific information is communicated, building transparent software and encouraging responsible approaches to digital health and information security.
