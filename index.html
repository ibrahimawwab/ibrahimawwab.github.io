About Ibrahim Awwab

I’m Ibrahim Awwab, also known as Muhammad Ibrahim Awwab. I’m an author and independent researcher from Afghanistan based in Greater London. I founded Awwab Academy to make medical laboratory science and cybersecurity education more accessible.

I write about laboratory diagnostics and digital health, and develop open-source tools that make it easier to trace evidence.
