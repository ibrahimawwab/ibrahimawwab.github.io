About Muhammad Ibrahim Awwab

I’m an independent researcher from Afghanistan based in Greater London, and I’m known professionally as Ibrahim Awwab. I founded Awwab Academy to make medical laboratory science and cybersecurity education more accessible.

I write about laboratory diagnostics and digital health, and develop open-source tools that make it easier to trace evidence.
