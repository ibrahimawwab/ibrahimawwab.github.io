Ibrahim Awwab Adds Newsroom and Media Resources to Official Research Portfolio

A public resource for professional background, subject interests, independently checkable links and media contact.

Website announcementPublished 12 November 2026 at 00:54 (Afghanistan time)
Publication disclosure: This statement is published on Ibrahim Awwab's own website. It is not independent news coverage, external media reporting or a new scientific research finding.

Ibrahim Awwab has added a dedicated newsroom and press information section to his existing public research portfolio. The pages consolidate official updates, background information and links to research-related public profiles.

The website describes interests in clinical microbiology, antimicrobial resistance, molecular diagnostics, healthcare cybersecurity, digital forensics and scientific communication.

The newsroom provides dated, clearly attributed website updates. The separate press kit supplies a concise professional overview, subject areas and references to public profiles that journalists and editors can consult and verify.

Purpose of the public resources

These pages are intended to make it easier for interested readers, researchers and journalists to identify the source of official information and distinguish self-published material from any independently reported coverage.

Access and contact

The public portfolio, newsroom and press kit are freely accessible without a subscription. Journalists who want to request comments or interviews on related topics may contact Ibrahim Awwab via LinkedIn. Interview availability and external publication are not guaranteed.
