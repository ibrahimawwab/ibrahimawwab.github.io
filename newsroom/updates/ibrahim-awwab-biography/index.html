Ibrahim Awwab’s Biography, Education and Research

A profile of the Afghan medical microbiologist, cybersecurity researcher and founder of Awwab Academy.

Professional biography18 November 2026By Ibrahim Awwab

Ibrahim Awwab has published an expanded professional biography on his official website. It brings together his education, independent research interests and public work in laboratory science, healthcare cybersecurity and education.

Muhammad Ibrahim Awwab, known professionally as Ibrahim Awwab, is an independent researcher from Afghanistan. His work and writing explore how reliable laboratory evidence and secure health information can support decisions in healthcare.

Education and research interests

Awwab’s public portfolio lists a PhD in Microbiology and an MSc in Clinical Microbiology from Queen Mary University of London, and an MSc in Cyber Security from the University of London.

His interests in clinical microbiology include infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance and molecular diagnostics. In cybersecurity, his interests include digital forensics, patient data privacy and the security of healthcare information systems. These areas meet in the way laboratory results are recorded, shared and used by clinical teams.

Awwab Academy and science communication

Awwab founded Awwab Academy to make medical laboratory science and cybersecurity education more accessible. He also publishes Awwab Scientific Review, a newsletter that covers clinical microbiology, laboratory science and technology.

His public articles explain topics such as how human DNA tests are performed and interpreted. He also writes for laboratories and health education teams and shares software work through GitHub.
